What we built

We established a complete design system for Olive & Vine — one olive hue, one typeface family, one signature gesture — so the brand never fragments across services, languages, or surfaces.

Letterspacing

Left at Roboto's default. The only deliberate tracking is the inherited tightness on the large hero display — no all-caps tracking, no expanded labels.

No italic as a pattern

Italic is never a recurring emphasis device. There is no display serif and no script — Roboto carries the entire visual identity on its own.

The discipline

What we deliberately left out

A system is defined as much by its restraint as its rules. These are the lines we hold.

No shadows

No elevation system, no drop shadows, no glow. Hover is communicated entirely through colour and the rounded corner.

No second hue

One olive green throughout. Hierarchy comes from lightness and opacity tiers — never from introducing a new colour.

No gradients or texture

Surfaces are flat. Backgrounds are photographs or solid colour — never abstract gradients or decorative patterns.

No emoji, no glyph icons

Every arrow, check and chevron is a drawn icon. Sentence case everywhere; the wordmark is the only exception.
